# HawkerSense
### From handwritten sales to a preparation plan

[← Back to Peter's portfolio](https://github.com/Ginnnyyyy)

**Context:** Hackathon project · **Stack:** Python, FastAPI, React, Firebase, Google Cloud AI

## The problem
A hawker's sales records can live in a notebook, making it harder to compare demand, track stock and decide how much to prepare.

## The approach
HawkerSense brings sales recording, stock management and demand forecasts into one workflow. A user can photograph a notebook page, review the extracted entries and confirm them before adding them to sales history. An AI assistant can query account tools and draft preparation guidance, with the hawker making the final decision.

## What the project demonstrates
- Turning an operational problem into a practical application.
- Combining structured records, numerical forecasting and conversational AI.
- Designing review steps around uncertain photo extraction and AI suggestions.
- Connecting a React interface to a Python API and managed cloud services.

## Status and limits
The project includes a deployed application and a sample workspace. The demonstration and benchmark data are generated; they do not establish real-world waste reduction. Forecasts have uncertainty, and supplier ordering is not automated.
