# Digital access & innovation
### Comparing Germany and Poland through business intelligence

[← Back to Peter's portfolio](https://github.com/Ginnnyyyy)

**Context:** University team project · **Focus:** Power BI modelling and dashboard development

## The question
How did internet use and computer technology patent activity change in Germany and Poland, and what can a dashboard reveal about their relationship?

## The approach
The project brings World Bank internet use and population figures together with European Patent Office computer technology application counts for 2018–2024.

The analytical table has 14 country-year observations. A country dimension and date dimension support consistent filtering. Nine visuals cover descriptive comparisons, diagnostic exploration and limited forward projections.

## My focus
Power BI implementation: the data model, DAX measures, interactive visuals, country/year filtering and forecast presentation.

## What the project demonstrates
- Modelling data at a clear country-year grain.
- Comparing patent activity using a per-million-residents measure.
- Building visuals around specific analytical questions.
- Distinguishing historical values, regression baselines and target scenarios.

## Interpretation limits
Internet use is a proxy for digital access, and one patent category covers only part of innovation. Fourteen observations support a small exploratory study. Association does not establish causation, and two-year projections are scenarios rather than reliable long-range predictions.
