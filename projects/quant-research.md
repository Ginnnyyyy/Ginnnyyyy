# Quantitative research
### Historical experiments with explicit accounting and validation

[← Back to Peter's portfolio](https://github.com/Ginnnyyyy)

**Context:** Personal research project · **Stack:** Python, offline tests, historical market data

## The question
How can a trading research workflow separate strategy assumptions, portfolio accounting and execution validation?

## The approach
The project studies equity and gold ETF historical scenarios with separate hypothetical portfolios. It includes replay logic, a durable journal, accounting checks and offline execution emulation.

## What the project demonstrates
- Keeping experiments and asset universes clearly separated.
- Checking settlement timing, distributions and portfolio accounting.
- Testing software with synthetic inputs and fake broker responses.
- Requiring prospective paper observations before stronger execution claims.

## Status and limits
Historical research and offline software checks are implemented. Broker paper acceptance and prospective observations remain unverified. Live trading is blocked. A passing software test or a historical result is not evidence of future profitability.

This overview describes engineering and research methods. Source, private data and account information are not published.
